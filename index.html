💰 Pension & Savings Tools

⚠️ Estimate only, not official. This uses the standard accrual formula for Nepal's pre-2076 (Legacy) civil service pension scheme. It does not account for caps, dearness allowance adjustments, or scheme-specific rules that may apply to you. For your actual pension amount, confirm with the Pension Management Office (pension.gov.np). This tool does not cover the Contributory Pension scheme (for those appointed after Shrawan 1, 2076) or the private-sector SSF scheme, which use different calculations entirely.

Legacy Government Pension (pre-2076 appointees)

SSF Contribution Calculator

Nepal's Social Security Fund — Employee 11% + Employer 20% = 31% of basic salary. Calculated on basic salary only (allowances excluded). Per Social Security Act 2074 & Regulations 2075.

SIP Calculator

Estimate the future value of a monthly Systematic Investment Plan.
