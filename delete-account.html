Delete your account

Last updated: October 2026

You can delete your Health Loksewa account and your data at any time. This page explains how, what is removed, and what to do if you cannot log in.

1. Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Health Loksewa and log in.
  2. Tap Profile, then open Account & Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm. For your security the app may ask for your password, or ask you to sign in with Google again.

Deletion starts straight away and cannot be undone.

2. What is deleted

  • Your profile and the list of your devices.
  • Your Leaderboard entry, and your Tournament and Battle scores.
  • Your Study Group posts, replies, shared notes and challenge results, your group memberships and join requests. A group you created is handed to another member, or deleted if you were its only member.
  • Your list of blocked users, and the reports you sent about content or people.
  • The study data saved for your account on that phone.
  • Your login (the email or Google sign-in used to access the app).

3. What may remain

  • Flash card decks you published to the Community are not linked to your account. If you want one removed, contact us (section 5).
  • Deck ratings, “helpful” votes and the AI usage record are anonymous and not linked to your account.
  • If you started a payment and did not finish it, a temporary reference to that attempt (your account ID and the amount) is cleared automatically within 7 days.
  • Payment records held by eSewa or Khalti follow their own policies.
  • Copies you downloaded or screenshots you took stay with you.

4. Only want to remove some data?

  • Delete my health data (Profile → Account & Privacy) removes the mood and health entries saved on your phone and keeps your account.
  • Download my data (same place) gives you a copy of your data before you delete anything.

5. Cannot log in? Ask us to delete your account

Send an email to healthloksewa026@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject Delete my account. If you signed up with Google, use that Google email address. We will confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

For everything we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.
