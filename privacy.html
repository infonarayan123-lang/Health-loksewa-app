Health Loksewa

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Health Loksewa ("the App") is an educational app for Nepal Loksewa health service exam preparation. This policy explains, in simple words, what information the App collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

1. Information you give us

  • Account: your full name, email address, and phone number (only if you type it when signing up; it is empty if you sign in with Google).
  • Password: handled by Google Firebase. It is stored in encrypted form and we can never see it.
  • Exams you choose, your notification settings, and your answers in the App.

2. Information saved with your account

  • Your Premium status and its start and end dates.
  • Daily usage counts for features with free limits (for example AI messages, Mock Tests and Past Question papers).
  • The date you last opened the App (used only to send study reminders), and a record that you accepted this policy.
  • My Devices: the type of device and browser you use (for example "Android, Chrome") and when it was last active, so you can see and log out your devices.
  • A notification token, if you turn notifications on, so we can send the notifications you chose.
  • Blocked users and Community Rules: if you block someone, we save who you blocked with your account, so the block works on all your devices and you are not matched with them in Quick Battle. We also save that you accepted the Community Rules.
  • Reports you send: if you report a post, reply, note, person or deck, we receive what you reported (up to 300 letters of its text, or of the AI answer you flagged), the reason, any note you add, your account ID and the time. Only we can read reports, and the person you report is not told who reported them. Reports you sent are deleted when you delete your account.

3. What other students can see

  • Your name, points and accuracy on the Leaderboard, and your scores in Tournaments and Battles.
  • In Study Groups you join: your name, your posts, replies and shared notes (visible to that group's members).
  • Flash card decks you choose to publish to the Community, with the creator name you type.

4. Information kept only on your phone

  • Your notes, bookmarks, mistakes, flash cards, study timer and similar study tools.
  • Mood and health logs (daily mood check-in, blood pressure, sugar, heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep, water, habits, gratitude notes) are saved only if you allow "Mood and health tracking". They stay on your phone and are not sent to our servers or to AI. You can turn this off anytime, and delete these entries anytime with Profile > Account & Privacy > Delete my health data.
  • How much is kept on your phone: the most recent 200 mood check-ins; the most recent 50 each of blood pressure, sugar, heart rate and blood oxygen readings; the most recent 30 sleep entries; the most recent 60 gratitude notes; the most recent 200 wrong questions. When a new entry is saved beyond these limits, the oldest is removed. The water and habit trackers keep small daily records until you delete them.

5. AI features

The AI Assistant, Notes AI, AI flash card generation, AI answer checking, and the "Ask AI" buttons send the text you type or choose (for example a question, a note or a news headline) to AI services to create an answer: Groq, Google Gemini and OpenRouter (OpenRouter may pass it to the AI model it selects). Your name, email and account details are not sent. We keep only a simple usage record (which service and feature was used, and whether it worked), not your text and not linked to your account. Please do not type private or sensitive personal information into AI features. AI answers can contain mistakes; always check important facts.

6. Payments

Premium payments are made through eSewa or Khalti. We receive only the confirmation and amount needed to turn on Premium. We never see or store your wallet password, PIN or card details.

7. Services we use

  • Google Firebase: login, account storage and notifications.
  • Google Sheets and Apps Script: the App's study content and its server. Content is backed up weekly.
  • Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter: AI features (see section 5).
  • eSewa and Khalti: payments.
  • YouTube: video lessons and live classes play from YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.
  • News websites: the News Portal shows headlines from public news websites; opening an article takes you to that website.

We do not use advertising or tracking services, and we never sell or rent your information.

8. How long we keep information

We keep your account information (including Premium dates and daily usage counts) while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed as described below. If you start a payment and do not finish it, a temporary reference to that attempt (your account ID and the amount) stays on our server until it is cleared. Published deck contents, deck ratings, "helpful" votes and the AI usage record are not linked to your account and may remain after you delete it. Information on your phone stays until you delete it, delete your account, or remove the App.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Change your name, reset your password, and turn notifications on or off in your Profile.
  • Turn "Mood and health tracking" on or off, and delete those entries, in Profile > Account & Privacy.
  • Delete your account yourself in Profile > Account & Privacy > Delete Account. This removes your profile, devices, blocked-users list, the reports you sent, Leaderboard entry, Tournament and Battle scores, your Study Group posts, replies and shared notes, your group memberships and join requests, and the data saved for your account on that phone. Groups you created are handed over to another member (or deleted if you were the only member).
  • Community flash card decks you published are not linked to your account. If you want one removed, contact us.

10. Security

We protect your information with Firebase login, database security rules that let each student change only their own data, checks on our server, and strong scrambling (hashing) of App Lock and Note Lock PINs. No system is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

11. Children

The App is meant for students preparing for Loksewa exams and is not directed at children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the App.
