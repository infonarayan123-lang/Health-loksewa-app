Community Rules

Last updated: October 2026

Study Groups, discussions, shared notes, published flash card decks and Battles let students talk to each other. To keep them safe and useful for exam study, everyone who uses them agrees to these rules.

1. Be respectful

No bullying, harassment, threats or insults. Disagree politely.

2. No hate

Do not attack or demean people because of their race, caste, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, region or sexual orientation.

3. No sexual or violent content

No nudity, sexual content, graphic violence, or content that encourages self-harm.

4. Protect children

Content that sexually exploits or endangers children is strictly forbidden. We remove it, close the account, and report it to the authorities where the law requires.

5. No spam, scams or advertising

Do not post adverts, referral links or requests for money.

6. Keep it about study, and keep it honest

Do not share other people's private information (phone numbers, addresses, photos). Do not post material you do not have the right to share. Do not post wrong answers on purpose to mislead others.

What happens if the rules are broken

We review reports. We may remove content, warn a student, or suspend or remove an account, without notice.

Your tools

Tap the ⋯ button next to a post, reply, shared note, member, deck or opponent to report it or block the person. Blocking hides their posts, replies and shared notes from you, and they will not be matched with you in Quick Battle. You can unblock anyone in Profile > Account & Privacy > Blocked users. To report something else, use Profile > Support and About > Report a problem or user.
